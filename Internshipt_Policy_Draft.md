## 1. PREAMBLE

1.1 Manipur Technical University (MTU), established on 23rd April 2016 under the Manipur Technical University Act, 2016, is committed to the implementation of the National Education Policy 2020 (NEP-2020), the University Grants Commission’s Guidelines for Internship/Research Internship for Undergraduate Students (2023), and the AICTE Internship Policy.  
1.2 The University believes that structured internship training is an essential instrument for integrating classroom knowledge with workplace demands, enhancing employability, fostering research aptitude, and developing professional ethics among students.  
1.3 This Policy applies to all full-time regular students of MTU across all programmes, departments, and affiliated institutions.  
1.4 The Policy distinguishes two parallel pathways:  
(a) internship at MTU’s own laboratories, departments, centres, and institutional projects; and  
(b) internship away from MTU — at industry, research laboratories, government organisations, NGOs, start-ups, academic institutions in India or abroad, or with recognised individual experts.

---

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **University / MTU** — Manipur Technical University, Imphal.  
2.2 **Student** — any bonafide full-time regular student enrolled in a programme approved by MTU.  
2.3 **Internship** — a structured, supervised, time-bound work or research engagement external to the classroom, undertaken for academic credit, professional exposure, or research training.  
2.4 **Research Internship** — an internship focused on ideation, experimentation, data collection, analysis, and scholarly dissemination under faculty/mentor guidance.  
2.5 **Internship Providing Organisation (IPO)** — any industry, R&D laboratory, government department, NGO, academic institution, start-up, cooperative, recognised expert, or MTU department offering an internship slot.  
2.6 **Internship Supervisor (IS)** — the person at the IPO responsible for day-to-day supervision of the intern.  
2.7 **Mentor** — a faculty member or approved expert from MTU assigned to monitor and grade the intern.  
2.8 **Nodal Officer** — a faculty or officer designated by MTU to coordinate, approve, monitor, and record all internships.  
2.9 **Internship Diary** — a bound/log record maintained by the student documenting daily activities.  
2.10 **Internship Report** — a structured final document submitted after completion.  
2.11 **Credit** — as defined by CCFUP/NHEQF: one credit equals approximately 30 hours of engagement per semester or 40–45 hours for summer/winter internships.

## 3. OBJECTIVES

The internship programme is designed to:

3.1 Integrate classroom and workshop learning with real-world workplace demands.  
3.2 Expose students to the culture, challenges, and expectations of professional environments.  
3.3 Develop technical competence, problem-solving, teamwork, and decision-making skills.  
3.4 Foster research aptitude, analytical thinking, and scholarly communication.  
3.5 Provide exposure to emerging technologies and industry-standard tools.  
3.6 Strengthen entrepreneurial mindset and awareness of enterprise formation.  
3.7 Cultivate professional ethics, integrity, and social responsibility.  
3.8 Build industry-academia-government-NGO partnerships for sustained internship opportunities.  
3.9 Support students in making informed career choices.  
3.10 Provide structured pathways for students from Northeast India to access national and international opportunities.

## 4. ELIGIBILITY AND TIMING

4.1 **Minimum Eligibility**

| Programme | Eligible After | Pre-Requisite Academic Standing |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------------------|
| B.Tech / B.Arch | 4th Semester | No backlog in prior semesters; minimum CGPA 6.0 or as specified by Department |
| M.Tech / MCA / MBA / M.Sc | 1st Semester completed | Minimum CGPA/SGPA as prescribed |
| Ph.D. | Pre-PhD coursework completed | As per Ph.D. Regulations of MTU |

4.2 **Internship Windows**

(a) **Summer Internship:** May–July (after 4th, 6th, or 8th semester depending on programme).  
(b) **Winter/Short Internship:** December–January.  
(c) **Semester-Long Internship / Thesis/Project:** 8th semester for 4-year programmes with research component.  
(d) **NOC-based off-season internship:** Permitted only with Vice-Chancellor / Dean’s approval on a case-to-case basis.

4.3 **Number of Internships**

(a) A student may undertake a maximum of **two** distinct internships during the entire programme.  
(b) Exceptions for research internships may be made by the Dean (R&D) with the Vice-Chancellor’s approval.

4.4 **Non-Academic Clash**

Internship must not conflict with scheduled classes, examinations, or laboratory practicals. Any clash must be resolved with the Head of Department (HoD) before approval.

## 5. CATEGORIES OF INTERNSHIP

5.1 **Internship for Enhancing Employability**
- Industry internships, corporate projects, government department attachments, MSME/start-up roles, NGO project roles, rural/community development assignments, innovation/entrepreneurship assignments.

5.2 **Research Internship**
- Faculty-guided research projects at MTU or external HEIs, R&D laboratory attachments, research under individual experts, scholarly field studies, patent/IPR-oriented research, dissertation/thesis work.

5.3 **Internship at MTU (Institutional)**
- Engagement in funded research projects, MTU Centre laboratories, incubation/start-up activities, institutional consultancy assignments, faculty research groups, MTU-industry joint projects.

5.4 **Internship Away from MTU (External)**
- Engagement at any IPO outside MTU, including international host institutions, subject to approval under Section 11.

## 6. STRUCTURING OF INTERNSHIP

6.1 **Credit Requirements (per CCFUP/NHEQF and AICTE norms)**

| Programme | Minimum Internship Credits | Approximate Duration |
|-----------|---------------------------|----------------------|
| B.Tech / B.Arch (4-year) | 14–20 credits over programme | 600–700 hours |
| B.Sc / BCA / BBA (3-year) | 2–4 credits | 60–120 hours |
| M.Tech / MCA / MBA / M.Sc | 4–8 credits | 160–320 hours |

6.2 **Credit-to-Hour Mapping**

(a) Semester-embedded internship: 1 credit = 30 hours per semester.  
(b) Summer/winter internship: 1 credit = 40–45 hours.  
(c) Dissertation/Research Project (8th semester): 12 credits = approximately one full semester of engagement.

6.3 **Internship Vertical / Sector Options (Indicative)**

MTU may facilitate internships across, but not limited to:
1. Agriculture and Allied Sectors  
2. IT/ITeS, Electronics, AI/ML, IoT, Cybersecurity  
3. Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Core Engineering  
4. Healthcare, Biomedical, and Life Sciences  
5. Financial Services, Banking, Insurance, FinTech  
6. Tourism, Hospitality, and Sports Management  
7. Public Policy, Governance, Legal Studies, Social Work  
8. Design, Architecture, Arts, Heritage, and Culture  
9. Renewable Energy, Environment, and Sustainability  
10. Rural Development, MSME, Cooperatives, and Start-ups  
11. Entrepreneurship, Innovation, and Incubation  
12. Defence, Space, and Strategic Technologies  
13. Northeast India Regional Development Projects  

6.4 **Duration Norms**

(a) Minimum: 4–6 weeks for summer internships.  
(b) Standard: 6–8 weeks for AICTE-mandated summer internships.  
(c) Extended: Up to 12 months for M.Tech/Ph.D.-level research internships with VC approval.  
(d) Internship shall generally be completed in one continuous spell unless otherwise approved.

## 7. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

### 7.1 Student
- Register on the MTU Internship Portal before applying.  
- Maintain the Internship Diary from the first working day.  
- Submit fortnightly progress reports to the Mentor.  
- Submit the final Internship Report, completion certificate, and supervisor evaluation form to the Nodal Officer.  
- Adhere to the code of conduct of the IPO and MTU.  
- Secure prior written approval for any change in host organisation, location, or timeline.  
- Notify MTU Nodal Officer immediately in case of termination, early completion, or extension.

### 7.2 Internship Providing Organisation (IPO)
- Provide a clearly defined internship role with measurable outcomes.  
- Nominate an Internship Supervisor (IS).  
- Provide necessary safety briefings, access, and supervision.  
- Issue a completion certificate/report with specific performance assessment.  
- Refrain from engaging interns in hazardous, illegal, or ethically questionable work.

### 7.3 Internship Supervisor (IS) at IPO
- Supervise day-to-day work and attendance.  
- Assess the intern’s performance fortnightly.  
- Issue a completion certificate and evaluation report at the end of the internship.  
- Coordinate with the MTU Mentor for any concerns.

### 7.4 Mentor at MTU
- Be a faculty member or approved expert from MTU.  
- Be assigned before the internship begins.  
- Review fortnightly progress reports.  
- Visit or contact the host organisation at least once during the internship (physical or virtual).  
- Grade the internship report and viva/presentation.  
- Submit evaluation to the Nodal Officer.

### 7.5 Nodal Officer
- Be designated by the Vice-Chancellor at the department or university level.  
- Maintain the MTU Internship Portal database.  
- Verify eligibility and issue the No-Objection Certificate (NOC).  
- Coordinate MOUs with host organisations.  
- Monitor compliance and flag anomalies.  
- Submit semester-wise internship reports to the Dean (R&D)/Registrar.

### 7.6 Head of Department (HoD)
- Approve semester-specific internship timelines.  
- Nominate Mentors for students of the department.  
- Oversee evaluation and grade submission.  
- Flag at-risk students to the Nodal Officer.

### 7.7 Dean (Research & Development) / Dean (Academics)
- Approve policy amendments.  
- Oversee the R&D Cell / Internship Cell.  
- Ensure AICTE/UGC compliance.  
- Approve exceptional cases.

### 7.8 Vice-Chancellor
- Final approving authority for policy and exceptional cases.  
- Authorise MOUs with national/international organisations.

## 8. PROCEDURE FOR INTERNSHIP

### 8.1 General Pre-Departure Checklist

Step 1 — Student identifies internship opportunity through:  
(a) MTU Internship Portal / AICTE Internship Portal / Internshala, or  
(b) self-application to an IPO.

Step 2 — Student submits an Internship Request Form (Annexure-A) to the Nodal Officer/HoD at least **4 weeks** before the start date.

Step 3 — Nodal Officer verifies eligibility and requests Mentors to be assigned.

Step 4 — HoD/Mentor reviews the role, host organisation, and learning outcomes.

Step 5 — MTU issues a No-Objection Certificate (NOC) (Annexure-B).

Step 6 — Student reports to the IPO on the approved start date.

Step 7 — Internship Diary is issued by the Nodal Officer and signed off by the IPO Supervisor.

Step 8 — Fortnightly reports are submitted to the Mentor.

Step 9 — On completion, the student submits the Internship Report, IS evaluation, and completion certificate.

Step 10 — Evaluation by Mentor and presentation/viva voce.

Step 11 — Grades/credits are awarded by the department examination committee.

### 8.2 Internship at MTU (Institutional)

(a) Student applies to the concerned department / R&D Cell / Centre.  
(b) Faculty PI or Centre-in-charge accepts the student as an intern.  
(c) No NOC to external organisation is required.  
(d) Internal Internship Approval Form (Annexure-C) is submitted to HoD.  
(e) Same evaluation and credit norms apply.

### 8.3 Internship Away from MTU (External)

(a) All external internships require prior written NOC from MTU.  
(b) Internships obtained through campus recruitment by MTU’s Training & Placement Cell (T&P) require T&P NOC instead of departmental NOC, but departmental NOC is still mandatory for credit.  
(c) Unauthorised internships — i.e., internships commenced without NOC — will not be counted for credit and may attract disciplinary action.

### 8.4 MOU-Based Internships

MTU may sign MOUs with IPOs to create dedicated internship slots. MOU-based internships follow a fast-track approval route with pre-approved mentors and supervisors.

## 9. CREDIT STRUCTURE AND EVALUATION

### 9.1 Credit Award

(a) Credits are awarded only after successful completion and evaluation.  
(b) Internship grades are on a standard scale (S/A/B/C/D/F) or as per MTU’s existing ordinance.  
(c) Grades shall appear on the grade card and contribute to CGPA/SGPA.

### 9.2 Evaluation Components and Weightage

| Component | Weightage |
|-----------|-----------|
| Internship Supervisor’s evaluation (from IPO) | 30% |
| Internship Diary and fortnightly reports | 20% |
| Final Internship Report quality, originality, innovation | 30% |
| Seminar presentation / Viva voce | 20% |

For research internships, add a component for **research output** (paper/poster/patent) weighted at 10%, reducing report weightage proportionately.

### 9.3 Evaluation Committee

(a) For each batch, the HoD shall constitute an Evaluation Committee consisting of:  
(i) Mentor (Chairperson),  
(ii) One faculty member from the same or allied specialisation,  
(iii) Internship Supervisor’s report as external input.  
(b) For 8th-semester research dissertations, the committee shall include at least one external examiner.

### 9.4 Repeat / Remedial Internship

If a student fails the internship, one repeat opportunity may be granted in the subsequent summer or semester, subject to time-to-degree limits.

## 10. INTERNSHIP AT MTU (INSTITUTIONAL)

10.1 MTU’s own departments, R&D Cell, Centres of Excellence, incubation centre, and funded projects may offer internship slots to students.  
10.2 A faculty member or PI must accept the student formally before the internship begins.  
10.3 The Mentor is the accepting faculty member or a designated faculty within the same Centre.  
10.4 Students working on externally funded faculty projects may be entitled to a monthly stipend from the project grant, as sanctioned by the PI and consistent with funding norms.  
10.5 Students on MTU institutional internships are bound by the same attendance and reporting norms as external internships.

## 11. INTERNSHIP AWAY FROM MTU (EXTERNAL)

### 11.1 Eligibility for External Internship

(a) Only students who have submitted the Internship Request Form and received MTU NOC are eligible.  
(b) Students on disciplinary probation are ineligible.  
(c) Students with outstanding dues to MTU must clear them before NOC issuance.

### 11.2 Approval of Host Organisations

(a) MTU maintains a panel of approved host organisations.  
(b) An IPO not on the panel requires the Nodal Officer to conduct due diligence — at minimum: registration/legal status check, nature of work offered, and safety assessment.  
(c) Students must not join an IPO that is not approved without NOC.  
(d) The Vice-Chancellor may blacklist any IPO found to exploit, endanger, or mislead students.

### 11.3 Types of External Host Organisations Permitted

(a) Industry and corporate houses — public and private sectors.  
(b) Government departments, autonomous bodies, PSUs, and local self-government bodies.  
(c) R&D institutions — CSIR, DRDO, ISRO, DBT, DST, BARC, ICMR, NEIST, and equivalent bodies.  
(d) Academic institutions — IITs, NITs, Central/State Universities, IIITs, IISERs, foreign universities.  
(e) MSMEs, start-ups, and incubators recognised by the Government of India or State Government.  
(f) NGOs, SHGs, cooperatives, and social enterprises.  
(g) Recognised individual experts with proven standing in their field.  
(h) International organisations — subject to VC approval and, where applicable, Government of India / MEA clearance.

### 11.4 NOC for Out-of-State / Out-of-Country Internships

(a) Students travelling outside Manipur for internship must submit travel itinerary, host address, and emergency contact details to the Nodal Officer.  
(b) For international internships, the student must obtain a No-Objection Certificate from MTU, visa endorsement copy, travel insurance, and parental/guardian consent.  
(c) The host institution abroad must be a recognised university, research laboratory, or established company.

### 11.5 Remote / Virtual Internships

(a) Remote internships are permitted provided the IPO is genuine, verifiable, and the role is substantive.  
(b) Students must maintain weekly video calls or evidence of engagement logs.  
(c) Final evaluation must include a demonstrable deliverable reviewed by both the external IS and MTU Mentor.

## 12. INTERNSHIP DIARY AND REPORT

### 12.1 Internship Diary

(a) The diary must be issued by the Nodal Officer and countersigned by the IS daily or weekly.  
(b) It must record: date, hours worked, tasks performed, tools/technologies used, learning outcome, and challenges faced.  
(c) Submissions of diary pages fortnightly to the Mentor are mandatory.

### 12.2 Internship Report

(a) Format: as prescribed in Annexure-D.  
(b) Must include: executive summary, organisation overview, objectives of internship, methodology, work performed, outcomes, learning reflections, and annexures.  
(c) Must carry original signatures of the IS and Mentor.  
(d) Plagiarism check is mandatory — similarity index not to exceed 15% (or as prescribed by MTU).

### 12.3 Completion Certificate

(a) Must be obtained from the IPO on the organisation’s letterhead.  
(b) Must state: student name, roll number, programme, dates of internship, role, nature of work, and performance assessment.

---

## 13. SAFETY, INSURANCE AND LIABILITY

13.1 MTU will not be liable for any injury, loss, or damage during external internships except where caused by MTU’s own negligence in approval or supervision.  
13.2 Students are strongly advised to:  
(a) take personal accident insurance and travel insurance before leaving for out-of-state / international internships;  
(b) carry emergency contact details;  
(c) comply with local laws, cultural norms, and health advisories of the host region;  
(d) inform the Nodal Officer immediately of any incident.  
13.3 For internships involving field work, construction sites, laboratories with hazardous materials, or conflict-zone regions, additional safety clearances from MTU and parental consent are mandatory.

---

## 14. GRIEVANCE AND DISCIPLINARY MECHANISM

14.1 Any grievance regarding internship allocation, evaluation, or conduct may be raised with the Nodal Officer.  
14.2 Unresolved grievances escalate to the HoD, then Dean (R&D), and finally the Vice-Chancellor.  
14.3 The following shall constitute misconduct and may attract penalties up to cancellation of credits, debarment from future internships, or disciplinary action under the MTU Ordinances:  
(a) Submitting fraudulent reports or forged completion certificates.  
(b) Commencing internship without NOC.  
(c) Plagiarism in the internship report.  
(d) Absence without approved leave during the internship period.  
(e) Breach of confidentiality of the host organisation.  
(f) Misconduct causing reputational damage to MTU or the host organisation.  
(g) Receiving stipend/compensation without disclosure to MTU where disclosure is required.

---

## 15. FINANCE, STIPEND AND FUNDING

15.1 Internships may be paid, unpaid, or partially reimbursed as determined by the IPO.  
15.2 Students are encouraged to seek funding from schemes such as:  
(a) AICTE Internship Portal opportunities,  
(b) DST-INSPIRE, CSIR-NET fellowships,  
(c) State Government schemes for Northeast students,  
(d) MTU own project grants or travel support,  
(e) External scholarships (e.g., ICCR, Erasmus+, overseas university grants).  
15.3 MTU may reimburse travel costs for internships availed through MTU MOUs or institutional arrangements, subject to availability of funds and prior approval.  
15.4 Stipends received by students belong to the student. No part thereof shall be claimed by MTU unless agreed in writing before the internship.

---

## 16. MONITORING AND RECORD-KEEPING

16.1 The Nodal Officer shall maintain a digital database of all internships — student-wise, year-wise, organisation-wise — accessible to the Registrar and Dean (R&D).  
16.2 An annual Internship Report shall be submitted to the Academic Council.  
16.3 MTU shall publish anonymised aggregate internship data in the University Annual Report.  
16.4 Each department shall submit an annual list of MOUs signed, internship slots created, and student placement outcomes.
